CISA — which helped local officials protect voting systems from hacking — has lost over 1,000 employees since early 2025. Trump's FY27 budget would eliminate CISA's election security program entirely, cutting its remaining 14 election security employees. In July, DHS began conditioning what election-security help remains on states running voter rolls through the SAVE database.
Courts Fighting Back
Supreme Court — Trump v. California (PENDING)
DOJ's emergency application asks SCOTUS to stay the injunction and allow full implementation of the mail-voting EO before Nov 3 — USPS mail-ballot gatekeeping and the DHS/SSA citizenship database. States' response due Aug 3, 4 p.m. The biggest open threat to the midterms now sits at the Court.
Filed Jul 27, 2026 · SCOTUSblog
1st Circuit — Mail-Voting EO Stays Blocked
Divided panel refused to stay Judge Talwani's injunction (Jun 25) blocking the EO's core for 2026, warning implementation "would sow confusion and threaten disenfranchisement of many eligible voters." Talwani also ruled Section 3 unconstitutional (Jul 23). The D.C. Circuit separately held a parallel challenge premature (Jul 28) — a circuit split as SCOTUS weighs the stay.
Jul 25, 2026 · CBS News · Votebeat
New Jersey — 18th Straight DOJ Loss
Judge Quraishi dismissed DOJ's suit for New Jersey's voter database, holding the Civil Rights Act doesn't cover it. DOJ is now 0 for 18 in district courts: CA, OR, MI, MA, RI, AZ, WI, ME, MD, PA, NH, NY, WV, NM, VA, CT, KY, NJ. No court has ruled for DOJ.
Jul 29, 2026 · Democracy Docket
6th Circuit — First Appellate Ruling Goes Against DOJ
Affirmed Michigan's dismissal: neither the NVRA nor HAVA compels states to hand over unredacted voter rolls with partial SSNs. Kentucky's district court followed the precedent Jul 23.
Jun 24, 2026 · Votebeat
Federal Appeals — DOJ Escalates to 8 Circuits
After losing everywhere, DOJ appealed its CT, NY, and NM losses (Jul 23), spreading the voter-data fight across the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 6th, 7th, 9th, and 10th Circuits. The 9th Circuit heard the CA/OR appeals May 19 — no ruling yet; Arizona's appeal is frozen pending the outcome.
Jul 23, 2026 · Democracy Docket
Supreme Court — Watson v. RNC: Grace Periods Upheld
5-4 ruling (Barrett writing) holds states may count mail ballots postmarked by Election Day but received after. Postmark grace periods in 14+ states — and military/overseas windows in ~30 — survive for 2026. The feared invalidation did not happen.
Jun 29, 2026 · Votebeat
Supreme Court — Callais Guts VRA Section 2
6-3 ruling struck Louisiana's second majority-Black district and held VRA compliance cannot justify race-conscious redistricting. Kagan in dissent: Section 2 is now "all but a dead letter." Triggered the Southern redistricting wave — LA, TN, FL, AL new maps; TX's mid-decade map separately upheld Apr 27.
Apr 29, 2026 · SCOTUSblog
Supreme Court — Alabama Map Reinstated on Shadow Docket
6-3 unsigned stay reinstated the map a three-judge panel had blocked as "intentionally racially discriminatory" — in use for the Aug 11 special primaries. First major shadow-docket application of Callais.
Jun 2, 2026 · Alabama Reflector
Tennessee — Memphis-Splitting Map Survives
Three-judge panel denied a preliminary injunction against the 9-0 map that splits the state's only majority-Black district across three districts. The map runs in 2026; the merits case continues.
Jul 23, 2026 · NBC News
Louisiana — Election Suspension Upheld
State judges denied every TRO against Gov. Landry's EO 26-038 suspending the May 16 House primaries mid-election — "the governor has the authority to suspend the upcoming election." Races reset: primaries Nov 3, general Dec 12.
May 1, 2026 · Democracy Docket
Georgia — Poll-Worker Subpoena Quashed · Ballots Stay with DOJ
Judge Ray (Trump appointee) quashed DOJ's grand jury subpoena for every Fulton 2020 election worker's personal information as an "arbitrary fishing expedition" (Jul 7). Earlier, Judge Boulee let DOJ keep the 700+ seized boxes while finding the FBI affidavit "misleading" (May 6).
Jul 7, 2026 · ABC News
Spring Dismissal Wave — RI, AZ, WI, ME, MD, PA, NH
Seven more voter-data cases dismissed between Apr 17 and Jun 29 (per the UW State Democracy Research Initiative tracker, corroborated by the Brennan Center's tracker). July added NY, WV, NM, VA, CT, KY, and NJ.
Apr–Jul 2026 · UW Tracker · Brennan Center
Federal — 23 States Sue Over Grant Conditions
Illinois-led coalition (23 states + D.C., plus the governors of KY and PA) challenges DHS's conditioning of $1.5B in Homeland Security grants on hand-marked ballots, manual audits, and SAVE voter-roll screening as unconstitutional coercion. At least $148M at stake.
Filed Jul 23, 2026 · Votebeat
Federal — National Voter Database Challenge Pending
Common Cause/LWV/ACLU suit to block DOJ's "surveil-and-purge" database (filed Apr 21) awaits a ruling on cross-motions for summary judgment. A separate LWV challenge to the mail-voting EO survived a motion to dismiss (Jun 18).
Apr 21, 2026 · Democracy Docket
